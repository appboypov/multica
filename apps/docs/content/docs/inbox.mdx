What concerns you sits on two pages in the sidebar:

- **Inbox** lists your to-dos: things that wait for you to act. Its number counts your open to-dos.
- **Updates** lists the news on the issues you follow: changes and comments that ask nothing of you. It has no number.

Neither is a full activity log; the full context stays on the corresponding issue.

## What lands in the inbox

Each to-do is one row, also when several are on the same issue. Overdue to-dos come first, then the newest. A to-do leaves by itself once it is handled:

| To-do | You get it when | It leaves when |
|---|---|---|
| **Question for you**, **Approval for you** | someone asks you a question or an approval | you answer it, it is no longer open, or it is reassigned to someone else |
| **Mentioned you** | someone @-mentions you in an issue description or comment, also through `@all` or `@squad` | you post any comment on that issue |
| **Assigned to you** | an issue assigned to you is in `todo`, `back_from_qa`, `blocked`, or `in_review` | the issue moves to another status or another assignee |
| **Waiting for your review** | an issue you created moves to `in_review`, and you are not its assignee | the issue leaves `in_review` |
| **Agent blocked** | an agent moves an issue to `blocked` in a run that works for you | the issue is no longer `blocked`, or an agent runs on it again |
| **Run failed** | an agent run that works for you fails | an agent runs on the issue again |
| **Autopilot paused**, **Quota limit reached**, **Quick create failed** | an autopilot pauses after failed runs or reaches its quota limit, or a quick create fails | the autopilot is resumed, an autopilot run is admitted again, or a retry creates the issue |

When an issue in `in_review` also has an open question for you, such as a merge approval, the Inbox shows only the question.

## Handle to-dos

Select a to-do to open it on the right. The line under its title says what makes it leave, and the body lets you act in place: answer the question, reply to the mention, change the status, or resume the autopilot.

**Mark done**, on the hovered row and at the top of the open to-do, removes the to-do at once and lowers the Inbox number. It doesn't change the issue. A later event, such as a new mention, opens a new to-do.

The Inbox has no read state and no Archived list: every row in it still waits for you. With no open to-dos, it says **Nothing waits for you** and offers **Open Updates**.

## What lands in Updates

Common notifications include:

- an issue is assigned to you, or moved away from you;
- an issue you subscribe to gets a new comment, or its assignee, status, priority, or dates change;
- an issue or comment you created gets a reaction;
- creating an issue through an agent completes;
- an autopilot you subscribe to creates a new issue.

Questions, mentions, failed runs, paused autopilots, quota limits, and failed quick creates are to-dos in your Inbox, not notifications in Updates.

Your own actions don't notify you. Multiple notifications on the same issue merge into one entry; open it to see the issue's recent changes.

## Sources of automatic subscription

Subscribing to an issue sends its news to your Updates. These participants subscribe to an issue automatically:

- the issue's creator;
- a new assignee;
- anyone who comments;
- anyone newly @-mentioned in the issue description;
- subscribers preconfigured in an autopilot.

Changing the assignee does not remove existing subscriptions. Subscribe or unsubscribe anytime on the issue page; after unsubscribing, ordinary updates stop reaching Updates, while a direct @-mention still gives you a to-do in your Inbox.

<Callout type="info">
When a sub-issue's status changes, subscribers of the parent issue get a notification in Updates as well. Sub-issue comments, priority, and date changes do not bubble up to the parent.
</Callout>

## Agents don't use the inbox

The Inbox and Updates are only for workspace members. Even when an agent is the assignee or a subscriber, it never gets to-dos or notifications.

@-mentioning an agent in a comment, assigning an issue to it, or triggering an autopilot creates a run directly instead of leaving the agent a to-do. `@all` gives to-dos to members only.

## Handle notifications in Updates

Selecting a notification marks it as read. Archive it when you're done; archiving doesn't modify or delete the corresponding issue.

Right-click a notification for **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread** and **Archive** (**Unarchive** in the Archived view). Mark as unread is the "I looked, but I'm not done with this" action: the notification goes back to unread and stays unread even while you keep it open — opening it again later marks it read as usual. Archived notifications keep their read state, so restoring one brings it back exactly as you left it.

From the Updates menu you can also:

- mark all as read;
- archive all, or archive all read;
- archive notifications for issues in `done` or `cancelled`;
- open Archived and restore a notification.

Open **Archived** at the bottom of Updates to browse older notifications. More records load as you scroll. Filters apply to the entire archive, including records that have not loaded yet.

## Tune notification types

Open **Settings → Notifications** to toggle each group of Updates notifications separately:

- assignments;
- status changes;
- comments;
- priority and dates.

Turning a group off doesn't stop the changes from being recorded on the issue — it only stops those notifications in Updates. To-dos ignore these settings: a question, a mention, or a failed run always lands in your Inbox. A few types, such as reactions, finished quick creates, and issues an autopilot creates, sit outside these four groups and are always delivered. System and browser notifications control desktop banners for to-dos and notifications alike: clicking a banner opens the to-do in the Inbox or the notification in Updates. They don't change either list.
